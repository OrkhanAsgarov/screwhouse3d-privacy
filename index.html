Screw House 3D – Privacy Policy

Developer: Oghuz Games · Effective date: October 25, 2026

Screw House 3D ("the game") is a puzzle game suitable for children and families. We take privacy seriously. This policy explains what information the game uses and how. In short: we do not collect, store or sell any personal information, and the game does not require an account.

1. Information we do not collect

The game does not ask for, collect or share any personal information. This includes names, email addresses, phone numbers, photos, contacts and precise location. There is no account, login, chat or any feature that lets players communicate with each other or share content.

2. Information stored on your device

To save your progress, the game stores the following on your own device only:

  • completed levels and stars;
  • coins, purchased tools and helpers;
  • daily reward progress;
  • settings (sound, music, vibration, day/night sky).

This information never leaves your device, and we cannot access it. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it permanently.

3. Advertising

The game shows two kinds of ads:

  • Rewarded ads (for example to get extra time, an extra box or double coins) are shown only when the player chooses to watch one by tapping a button. The game can be played without ever watching a rewarded ad.
  • Short full-screen ads may appear between levels (not during the first levels, and then only after every few completed levels). They can be closed by the player.

Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which participates in Google Play's Families Self-Certified Ads SDK Program. We configure AdMob as follows:

  • all ad requests are marked as directed to children (child-directed treatment);
  • personalized (interest-based) advertising and remarketing are turned off;
  • ad content is limited to the "G" (general audiences) rating;
  • the game does not use or request the Android advertising ID.

To deliver ads, AdMob may process limited technical information, such as IP address, device type, operating system version and ad interactions. This is used only to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, and not to build a profile of the player. For more information, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Permissions

The game uses the following Android permissions:

  • Internet: needed to load ads.
  • Vibration: for short haptic feedback, which can be turned off in Settings.

The game does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, photos or location.

5. Children's privacy

The game is designed to comply with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU/UK GDPR rules for children, and Google Play's Families Policy. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe that personal information about a child has been shared with us, please contact us and we will delete it.

6. In-app purchases

The game does not contain in-app purchases with real money. Coins are earned only by playing.

7. Changes to this policy

If we change how the game handles information, we will update this page and its effective date before the change takes effect.
